Athira Krishnan R

Hi, I am a Athira Krishnan R, a curious and passionate robotics engineer. I love exploring the intersection of robotics, AI, and computer vision to create intelligent systems that can navigate and interact with the world around them.

I am currently a PhD student in dept of AI at IIT Hyderabad, working under the guidance of Prof. Sumohana S Channappayya and Prof. K Sri Rama Murty. My research focuses on developing advanced AI based solutions to problems faced in robot navigation, particularly in the areas of multimodal intelligence. My PhD research aims to enhance the capabilities of robots to perceive, understand, and navigate complex environments using a combination of visual and linguistic inputs. My current research are funded by RCI, Govt. of India.

I also serve IEEE as a member of Professional Execom for IEEE Robotics and Automation Society (IEEE RAS) Kerala Chapter since 2021. Currently I hold there the position of Treasurer and GSAC Advisor.

Previously, I worked with classical approaches for path planning and navigation in mobile robots during my Master's at CET. I have also worked with TCS RnI Labs (On leave), where I explored deep learning techniques for a bunch of problems in image, text, audio and EEG data. At TCS, I contributed to projects involving image classification, sentiment analysis, and speech recognition, gaining hands-on experience in applying deep learning models to real-world problems.
